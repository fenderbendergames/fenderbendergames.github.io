Privacy Policy for Fender's Little Farm

Effective November 13, 2026 · replaces the policy of October 8, 2026

Fender's Little Farm is a game made by FenderBender Games ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with information. The short version: we don't collect any personal information from anyone, including children. The game has optional ads from Google, and those ads are set up for children: they aren't personalized and they're rated G.

What we collect

Nothing. The game doesn't ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or any other personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no analytics of our own and nothing to buy. We never receive any information about you or how you play.

Optional ads

The game offers bonus rewards, like extra time against a boss or double coins, for watching a short ad. An ad only plays when you tap a button to watch one. The game never shows an ad on its own.

  • The ads come from Google AdMob. Every ad request tells Google the game is directed to children, so Google shows only non-personalized ads rated G (suitable for all ages), with no interest-based ads and no remarketing.
  • To show and count ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ad software in the game collects some information automatically: the device's IP address (which can show its rough location), how the ads are viewed and tapped, information about how the app and the ad software are working, and device identifiers such as the app set ID. The game doesn't use the Android advertising ID. This information goes to Google, encrypted, and is handled under Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and its rules for apps directed to children. We don't receive it.
  • You can turn the ads off: in the game's Settings, switch off "Optional ad rewards". The ad buttons disappear, and the game doesn't load Google's ad software at all.

Your farm's save

Your progress is saved only on your device, and we never see it.

  • If you use "Back up your farm" in the game's Settings, the game turns your farm into a code. The code holds only game progress (levels, coins, crew and settings), and you decide where to keep it.
  • If your phone backs up its apps to your Google account, Android may include the game's save in that backup. That backup is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy, not by us.

Reminders and vibration

  • Phone reminders are off unless you turn them on in the game's Settings. They're scheduled on your device, and nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings.
  • The game uses your phone's vibration for tap feedback. You can turn it off in the game's Settings.

Children

Fender's Little Farm is made for families, including children under 13. We don't collect personal information from anyone, so we don't collect any from children. The game's ads follow Google Play's Families policy: they come from Google AdMob, which is certified for apps directed to children, and are set to be non-personalized and rated G. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Google Play

The game is downloaded from Google Play, which is run by Google under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your information

Everything the game stores is on your device. "Erase this farm" in the game's Settings deletes your progress, and uninstalling the game removes all of its data from your device. For information Google's ad software collected, see Google's privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we'll update this policy before that update is released, and change the date at the top.
