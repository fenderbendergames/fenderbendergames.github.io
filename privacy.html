Privacy Policy for Fender's Little Farm

Effective October 8, 2026

Fender's Little Farm is a game made by FenderBender Games ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with information. The short version: the game doesn't collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game doesn't ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no analytics or tracking, no ads and nothing to buy. It doesn't send any information about you or how you play over the internet.

Your farm's save

Your progress is saved only on your device, and we never see it.

  • If you use "Back up your farm" in the game's Settings, the game turns your farm into a code. The code holds only game progress (levels, coins, crew and settings), and you decide where to keep it.
  • If your phone backs up its apps to your Google account, Android may include the game's save in that backup. That backup is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy, not by us.

Reminders and vibration

  • Phone reminders are off unless you turn them on in the game's Settings. They're scheduled on your device, and nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings.
  • The game uses your phone's vibration for tap feedback. You can turn it off in the game's Settings.

Children

Fender's Little Farm is made for families, including children under 13. Because the game doesn't collect personal information from anyone, it doesn't collect any from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Google Play

The game is downloaded from Google Play, which is run by Google under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your information

Everything the game stores is on your device. "Erase this farm" in the game's Settings deletes your progress, and uninstalling the game removes all of its data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if optional ads are added in a future update), we'll update this policy before that update is released, and change the date at the top.
